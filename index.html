Baked in small batches, gone by the weekend

Sweet and savoury favourites are turned out in small batches — custom cakes, cupcakes and baked goodies made to order — and it's the kind of baking that disappears fast, so please order at least 2 days in advance on WhatsApp, then pick your pickup or delivery date in the cart.

how to order

Three steps to fresh bakes

Add everything you want to your cart, check the total, then send it all in a single WhatsApp message — no need to order one item at a time.

one

Add to cart

Head to the menu, tap an item to open it, pick any flavours or fillings it offers, then tap Add to cart and set your quantities with + / −.

two

Review & send

Open your cart, add your name and phone number, choose Pickup or Delivery, pick your date in the calendar — at least 2 days ahead — and add your address if delivering, then tap Send order on WhatsApp.

three

Collect or get it delivered

There's no fixed pickup or delivery day — your order is baked fresh for the date you pick in your cart. Pick up in St. Augustine, or have your order delivered. Ang Bakes confirms everything with you on WhatsApp.
